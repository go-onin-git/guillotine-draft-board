Week 5 Previews

Eight left, and Wednesday already hurt.
Skinner is gone. Eight teams remain. The first real bye week has arrived, the waiver market has redistributed another chunk of the dead, and several of the league’s best players have made “questionable” the most important word on the board. Yahoo’s Wednesday projections put Max and TJ at the top and Jayel and Colin at the bottom. Those numbers are useful, but not authoritative: Ja’Marr Chase is in concussion protocol, Saquon Barkley is dealing with a hamstring injury, Justin Jefferson is attempting to return from an ankle injury, and Lamar Jackson’s ankle is being monitored. This preview assumes every manager makes the best reasonable lineup changes before kickoff.
Post-waiver edition: Wednesday, October 7, 2026. News checked that morning against Yahoo and its transaction log. Injury status and Yahoo projections will change during the week; these previews assume sensible final lineups. The elimination percentages are editorial estimates, not model outputs. They total 100% and reflect injury uncertainty, roster depth and likely lineup optimization—not just Yahoo’s current starters.
The waiver report

The headline is TJ buying the two receivers Skinner had only just acquired. Nico Collins cost $211, beating Colin ($100), Pete ($79), Matt ($75) and Max ($63). Christian Watson cost another $120, beating Pete’s $75. That is $331 spent, but it converts the league’s largest cash pile into two playable receivers while leaving $563—the largest remaining budget.

Pete won Malik Nabers for only $79. Colin and Matt both bid $50, making this the cleanest value of the auction if Nabers is healthy enough to supply his usual volume. Pete later added Parker Washington and dropped Jordan Addison.

Jayel paid $89 for Trevor Lawrence, beating Max’s $65. It is an important stabilizer for a roster that had Brock Purdy and Geno Smith as its current quarterbacks, but Jayel is now completely out of FAB.

Matt paid $165 for Emanuel Wilson. Skinner’s posthumous $0 bid was the only other offer. Matt paid for a usable back and, more importantly, kept one away from seven shrinking rosters.

The morning free-agent round continued after waivers:

  • TJ added Kyle Monangai for David Montgomery, then Aaron Jones for Rico Dowdle.
  • Pete added Parker Washington for Jordan Addison.
  • Max added Kyler Murray for Marcus Mariota.
  • Shoes added Deshaun Watson for Cam Ward.

Those $0 moves matter. At this stage, bench churn is not housekeeping; it is survival inventory.

The Week 5 read

This is the first week in which the bottom team on Wednesday is not obviously the worst team in the league. Jayel and Colin are the danger pair, with Pete and Millar close enough that one bad early window changes everything. TJ and Max have the clearest paths to safety, but Chase’s concussion is a reminder that no projection survives contact with Sunday.

The best roster may die. The richest roster may wait too long. And somewhere, Skinner is watching Nico Collins start for the man who needed a miracle to kill him.
