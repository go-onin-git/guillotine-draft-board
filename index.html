Week 8 Previews

Pete bought the estate. Millar found Jeanty on the curb. TJ kept the biggest wallet — and then grabbed Olave for free.
Medium Salsa went into Monday projected for 127.89 and came out chopped at 101.92. Jalen Coker’s 1.40, Bryce Young’s 7.42 and Chris Olave’s 8.60 left too much ground to recover. Max’s departure put Josh Allen, Jahmyr Gibbs, Trey McBride, Drake London, Ashton Jeanty and the rest of the roster into the deepest player pool of the season.

Pete attacked it hardest. Fist full of Hog paid $100 for Gibbs, $5 for London and $0 for Colston Loveland. Colin paid $41 for McBride. TJ paid $125 for Allen, then added Olave as a free agent and released Tetairoa McMillan. The surprise came after waivers: nobody submitted a winning bid for Jeanty, so Millar added him for free and dropped MarShawn Lloyd.

The auction has also exposed the endgame economy. The five survivors have only $135 left between them. TJ owns $87 of it. Colin has $28, Pete has $19, Millar has $1 and Jayel has none.

Yahoo’s untouched lineup totals put Jayel at 115.23 and Millar at 115.83. Those numbers need one important repair: Jayel currently has Isaiah Likely on bye in the TE slot, and a normal free-agent replacement such as Dalton Kincaid projects for 9.05. Colin also has two better waiver-era players on his bench: McBride over Sam LaPorta and Chase Brown over Ollie Gordon. With those expected Sunday moves, the danger board becomes Millar 115.83, Jayel 124.28, Colin 130.14, Pete 136.27 and TJ 137.45.

Five teams. Four advance. There is finally room between the floorboards.
