The strongest prediction is that TJ has the means to come away with a major upgrade. The least certain prediction is the clearing price on the second premium player. Pete can rationally chase Gibbs, McBride or several smaller improvements. Colin can rationally focus most of his $69 on McBride. Neither needs to behave irrationally for the auction to produce an inconvenient result for somebody else.

Pete’s important choice: Gibbs raises the ceiling; McBride fills the immediate TE vacancy; a cheap TE keeps most of the budget free. Allen improves an already strong QB pair by far less than he improves TJ’s.

Colin’s important choice: spend on the largest marginal gain. McBride adds more to this week’s projected starters than Jeanty or London. A spread of modest bids may buy depth while losing the one clear upgrade.

Millar and Jayel’s important choice: submit fallback claims across the useful leftovers. Millar’s $1 and first priority have actual value in the $0 market. Jayel needs enough TE alternatives that a single lost claim does not become a weekend problem.
