What decided the week

  • Colin’s best week of the season: Tee Higgins caught 10 passes for 155 yards and two touchdowns, good for 32.50. Jordan Love added 22.52, Jaylen Warren 20.80, and Puka Nacua 19.50. Colin was the only team over 150.
  • Jayel had the week’s best running back: Bhayshul Tuten scored 28.90. Michael Wilson added 22.80, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba had 20.80. Jayel survived comfortably despite only 4.46 from Jacoby Brissett in superflex.
  • TJ got the big performances and the wrong supporting cast: Ja’Marr Chase scored 25.60 and Brock Bowers 24.70. Kenneth Walker and Bijan Robinson combined for 18.00. Caleb Williams (13.98), D’Andre Swift (26.90) and Matthew Golden (17.40) were left on the bench; this time the cushion made it a hindsight story rather than a near-death experience.
  • Pete had Joe Burrow’s 42.48 and Jahmyr Gibbs’ 23.80, then ran into a wall of small numbers: Drake London scored 1.30, Tyler Warren 3.80, Javonte Williams 6.20 and CeeDee Lamb 9.30. Bucky Irving’s 21.00 was on the bench.
  • Millar had the only score below 100, and the only score below 120 by more than 40 points. There was no one-player villain. Flowers’ zero was painful, but the bye-week pileup and the low production from his remaining backs and receivers did the real damage.
