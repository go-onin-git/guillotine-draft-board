Week 6 Previews

Shoes went out with a feast. The league showed up with containers. We came for the leftovers. Millar left with McCaffrey.
Kenneth Walker, once a $598 purchase, went for $217 this time. TJ beat Max’s $201 and Pete’s $198, got his Week 6 running back, and kept $343 for the rest of the season. He also added Jameis Winston for $3 as insurance for Caleb Williams. Millar went the other direction. He put $392 of his $393 on Christian McCaffrey and has one dollar left. Pete took home three pieces of the estate: CeeDee Lamb for $198, Javonte Williams for $103 and Davante Adams for $68. Colin spent $50 on Chase Brown; Max added Bryce Young for $5 and picked up Woody Marks for free. The unedited Yahoo lineup totals missed three obvious Sunday fixes: Colin’s empty tight-end slot, Matt’s empty receiver slot, and Millar’s $392 McCaffrey claim still sitting on his bench. I also moved Jayel’s higher-projected Barkley into the lineup if he is active. With sensible Sunday lineups, the seven projections bunch between 118.97 and 122.47. The survival call is much less lopsided than the raw Yahoo board made it look.

The Monday arithmetic

Shoes had 59.80 before Adams; Matt had 81.50 before Stafford. That was a 21.70 gap, with both players active. Adams needed to outscore Stafford by more than 21.70 to pass Matt. A fixed Sunday target of roughly 36 points for Adams was only an estimate dependent on Stafford’s eventual score, not a guaranteed escape number.

Stafford scored 27.32; Adams scored 19.60. The gap grew to 29.42. To clear Matt’s final total, Adams would have needed more than 49.02. To clear Millar’s final 95.26 instead, he needed more than 35.46. He achieved neither.

Shoes’ best hindsight lineup would have swapped Daniel Jones’ 11.08 for Darnold’s 10.30, Ferguson’s 3.40 for Fannin’s 0.90, and Doubs’ 2.10 for Lamb’s 1.90: 82.88, still 12.38 below Millar. This was not a single bench mistake that changed the elimination.
