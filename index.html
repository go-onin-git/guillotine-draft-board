What decided the week

  • TJ had the week’s best roster and the best week. Kenneth Walker III scored 34.30, Ja’Marr Chase 26.50, Bijan Robinson 23.70 and Christian McCaffrey 21.70. That quartet alone produced 106.20. Christian Watson and Matthew Golden combined for 54.00 from the bench, so the Badgers had a luxury surplus as well as the highest score.
  • Colin’s quarterbacks kept the week comfortable. Jordan Love scored 36.18, Puka Nacua had 28.00, Dak Prescott added 19.50 and Tee Higgins had 17.70. Colin was the second-highest scorer, though Love’s 36.18 will be harder to replace if the team’s Week 10 lineup needs another option.

  • Jayel had no single catastrophic starter; the small scores stacked up. Purdy and Kelce were the only starters over 15. Six other starting spots produced 13.58 or fewer. His bench could not cover that kind of spread.
